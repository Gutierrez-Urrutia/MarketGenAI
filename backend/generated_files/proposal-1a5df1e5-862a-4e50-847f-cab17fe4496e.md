### 1. Executive Summary

This proposal outlines a comprehensive strategy for **Demo Client** to transition from a manual order-taking process to a fully automated, digital ordering ecosystem. By implementing a custom-branded mobile app, online web ordering portal, and integrated CRM, we aim to reduce operational bottlenecks, increase average order value (AOV), and capture lost revenue from busy hour drop-offs. Our projected outcome is a **20% increase in digital orders** and a **15% reduction in staff overhead** related to phone orders within the first 90 days.

### 2. Client Problem

**The Issue:** Demo Client is experiencing significant operational friction due to manual order management.

- **High Volume of Phone Calls:** During peak hours (12-1 PM and 6-8 PM), staff are overwhelmed with incoming calls, leading to long wait times, order errors, and frustrated customers.
- **Order Abandonment:** Customers who cannot get through via phone often abandon their order and choose a competitor.
- **Data Silos:** There is no centralized database of customer preferences, order history, or contact information, making targeted marketing (e.g., "re-order your usual") impossible.
- **Staff Burnout:** Employees are spending 40% of their shift on the phone instead of preparing food or serving in-store customers.

### 3. Proposed Solution

**Project Name:** "Pizza on Autopilot"

We will implement a **3-Pillar Automation System**:

1.  **Branded Mobile App (iOS & Android):** A user-friendly app for easy ordering, payment, and loyalty tracking.
2.  **Web Ordering Widget:** A seamless "Order Now" button integrated directly into the existing website.
3.  **Backend Integration:** A central dashboard that connects the app, web orders, and the POS system to eliminate double data entry.

### 4. Campaign Strategy

To drive adoption of the new automation system, we will launch a **"Skip the Line, Get the Slice"** campaign.

- **Phase 1: The Hook (Week 1-2)**
    - **In-Store Signage:** "Order on the app and get a FREE Garlic Knots with your first purchase."
    - **Staff Incentive:** Staff receive a $1 bonus for every customer they help download the app.
- **Phase 2: The Push (Week 3-4)**
    - **Email/SMS Blast:** Send a "Welcome to the Future" email to any existing email list, offering a 15% discount on the first automated order.
    - **Social Media:** Short video ads showing the "Pain" (busy phone) vs. the "Gain" (tapping order on phone).
- **Phase 3: Retention (Week 5+)**
    - **Loyalty Program:** "Buy 10 Pizzas, Get 1 Free" tracked exclusively through the app.
    - **Push Notifications:** "It's Friday! Your usual Pepperoni is ready in 15 minutes."
