<h2 style="margin-top:35px">Executive Summary</h2>
<p>Bloom & Co. Skincare is a premium ecommerce brand specializing in natural skincare products. To scale revenue, recover lost sales, and deepen customer loyalty, the company requires a sophisticated marketing automation system.</p>
<p>This proposal outlines a comprehensive AI-driven solution designed to create personalized customer journeys across email, SMS, and WhatsApp. Our approach will transform your customer relationships into measurable business growth.</p>
<p>By implementing automated workflows for welcome sequences, cart recovery, post-purchase engagement, and reactivation, Bloom & Co. Skincare will achieve higher conversion rates and a stronger competitive advantage.</p>

<h2 style="margin-top:35px">Problems Identified</h2>
<ul>
<li>High rate of abandoned shopping carts leading to significant revenue leakage.</li>
<li>Lack of personalized onboarding for new customers, reducing lifetime value.</li>
<li>No structured post-purchase follow-up, limiting repeat sales and brand advocacy.</li>
<li>Inactive customer base with no automated reactivation strategy.</li>
<li>Manual, fragmented communication channels failing to deliver a cohesive brand experience.</li>
</ul>

<h2 style="margin-top:35px">Proposed AI Solution</h2>
<p>Our solution deploys a dedicated team of four specialists: one marketing automation expert, one CRM specialist, one content writer, and one performance analyst. This team will design and implement a fully automated, multi-channel marketing engine.</p>
<p>The core of the proposal is the configuration of intelligent, trigger-based workflows. New customers will receive a tailored welcome sequence. Users who abandon their cart will be re-engaged through a timed series of email, SMS, and WhatsApp messages. Post-purchase journeys will nurture loyalty and encourage repeat orders, while a reactivation flow will win back dormant customers.</p>
<p>Each workflow will leverage AI for message personalization, optimal send-time optimization, and performance analysis, ensuring every interaction is relevant and effective.</p>
