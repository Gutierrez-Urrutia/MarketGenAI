### 1. Executive Summary

[Your Agency Name] proposes a comprehensive marketing and digital strategy for **Demo Client's Pizzeria** focused on driving adoption of a new **automated ordering app**. The current market shows a high demand for convenience; however, Demo Client is experiencing operational bottlenecks and missed revenue due to manual order taking.

This proposal outlines the strategy, deliverables, and timeline to achieve a seamless, high-volume ordering ecosystem.

### 2. Client Problem

Demo Client is facing a critical operational challenge:

- **High Volume of Phone Orders:** The kitchen is overwhelmed during peak hours (12 PM–1 PM & 7 PM–9 PM) due to manual order entry, leading to errors and long wait times.
- **Lost Revenue:** Customers often hang up due to busy signals or long hold times, resulting in an estimated **15% loss in potential daily sales**.
- **Data Silos:** There is no centralized customer database. Repeat customers cannot be identified, preventing targeted upselling or loyalty rewards.
- **Inefficiency:** Staff time spent on the phone could be better utilized for food preparation and quality control.

### 3. Proposed Solution

We propose the launch of **"Pizza Tap"** – a branded mobile ordering app designed for speed and simplicity.

**Core Features:**
- **Smart Menu:** High-resolution images with customizable toppings (size, crust, extras).
- **Saved Profiles:** One-tap reorder for returning customers.
- **Real-time Tracking:** Push notifications from "Order Received" to "Out for Delivery."
- **Loyalty Engine:** Points-based system (e.g., 1 point per $1 spent, redeemable for free toppings or sides).

**Integration:**
The app will sync directly with the Pizzeria’s existing POS system to eliminate manual data entry.

### 4. Campaign Strategy

We will use a three-phase "Zero Friction" strategy to drive downloads and habitual use.

**Phase 1: Pre-Launch Buzz (Weeks 1-2)**
- **In-Store Signage:** Table tents and window decals advertising "Skip the Line – Order Ahead."
- **Teaser Social Media:** Countdown posts on Instagram/Facebook showcasing app mockups.
- **Staff Training:** Scripts for staff to verbally invite customers to download the app for a free drink on their first order.

**Phase 2: Launch & Incentivize (Weeks 3-4)**
- **First Order Discount:** "Download now and get **$5 OFF** your first app order."
- **Referral Program:** "Share with a friend – you both get a free slice."
- **Geo-fenced Ads:** Google/Facebook ads targeting users within 3 miles of the pizzeria.

**Phase 3: Retention & Habit (Weeks 5-12)**
- **Push Notification Strategy:** Weekly "Flash Deals" (e.g., "50% off large pizzas from 2-4 PM").
- **Email Automation:** Welcome sequence, birthday rewards, and "We Miss You" offers for dormant users.
- **Review Harvesting:** In-app prompt after 3rd order: "Rate your experience."
